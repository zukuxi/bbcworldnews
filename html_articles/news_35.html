In shattered Cali, residents search for loved ones and signs of hope

Most deaths were reported in Cali, Colombia’s third-most populous city, as crews search collapsed buildings for those still trapped. At least 169 people have been killed and dozens more injured after a magnitude-7.4 quake hit western Colombia.
