Watch: Moment powerful earthquake shakes Colombia and crumbles buildings

A powerful 7.4 magnitude earthquake has shaken western Colombia. At least 111 people have been killed and dozens more are injured, according to local officials. Buildings have collapsed and rescue efforts are under way to free those who have been trapped beneath rubble.

The shaking was felt across the country, as well as in neighbouring Venezuela, Ecuador and Panama.
