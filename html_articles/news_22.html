What we know about the Pakistan avalanche and missing climbers

Seven climbers are still missing on Pakistan's Broad Peak after an avalanche on Thursday.

The seven are part of a group of 10 climbers that were in the area when the disaster struck.

The remains of Nadhira Ahmed Abdullah Al Harthy from Oman, Pur Bahadur Gurun from Nepal and Mallory Geis from the US have been found.

The rescue operation continues on Saturday.

The BBC's Caroline Davies explains what we know so far.
