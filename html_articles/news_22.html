Pomp and toasts: Day 2 of Trump and Xi in DC

US President Donald Trump held talks with President Xi Jinping at the White House on the second day of the Chinese leader's visit to Washington DC.

There was pomp and ceremony, but little in the way of confirmed progress on key issues.

The day ended with a state dinner, which was attended by major tech CEOs including Tesla's Elon Musk and OpenAI's Sam Altman.
