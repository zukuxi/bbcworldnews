Watch: Inside court as Lindsay Clancy's lawyer asks judge to remove juror

The jury in the Lindsay Clancy case failed to reach a verdict on their sixth day of deliberations.

Clancy's lawyer asked the judge to remove a juror after the jury forewoman sent a court note saying one holdout would not follow the court's instructions on reasonable doubt.

The defence's request to remove the juror was denied by the judge.

The BBC's Ana Faguy breaks down how the dramatic day in court unfolded.
