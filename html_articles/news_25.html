Another White House renovation? A look at Trump's new helipad

US President Donald Trump has made another addition to the White House: a helipad. The facility is the first dedicated landing pad at the White House. The project's completion comes after the Supreme Court allowed the president to continue construction of the $400m (£297m) ballroom project.

The BBC's Bernd Debusmann Jr takes a closer look.
