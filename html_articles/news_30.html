BBC breaks down video of the aftermath of New York City ICE shooting

US Immigration and Customs Enforcement shot and wounded a man in his car in New York City on Thursday while his five-year-old son was in the back seat. Body cam footage released by the New York Police Department on Friday, shows its officers arriving on scene and helping the man. The BBC’s Nada Tawfik breaks down the newly released footage of the aftermath of the shooting.
