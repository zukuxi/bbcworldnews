Watch: Trump says ban on some news outlets is 'because they're fake news'

The US president on Friday announced he is "immediately" banning CNN, MS NOW and Politico from the White House.

During an Oval Office event, he accused the three outlets of being "fake news" and "dishonest".

It is unclear what exactly a ban would entail, and whether employees of those outlets would be prevented from entering the White House grounds.
