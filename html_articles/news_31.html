Watch: BBC tracks down abusive teacher secretly released early

BBC Eye has discovered a teacher who was imprisoned for sexually assaulting a student in Indonesia has been secretly released early.

Other students have also made allegations against Mochamad Subchi Azal Tsani, known as Bechi, including Ira, who says he raped and sexually abused her for years.

The BBC put the allegations to Bechi - he did not respond.

The Ministry of Immigration and Correction said he was given early parole due to legal sentence reductions.
