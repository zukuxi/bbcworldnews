Watch: Pupils heard 'cracking' sound heard moments before Pakistan school roof collapse

More than 30 pupils and a teacher have been killed after a school roof collapsed in the north-western Pakistani city of Peshawar, according to hospital officials.

More than a dozen others are injured, with some in a critical condition. Several children are feared to be trapped under the rubble.

BBC Urdu's Azizullah Khan reports from Peshawar.
