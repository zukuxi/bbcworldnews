Watch: Moment Indonesian sneaker shop is torn apart by explosion

CCTV has captured the moment a sneaker shop was torn apart by an explosion in the San Antonio area of Surabaya, Indonesian authorities have confirmed.

Police have said five people have been hospitalised following the incident that was “strongly suspected” to be the result of a gas leak, and no suspicious substances or explosives have been found at the site.

They added that at least 41 buildings had been damaged by the explosion.
