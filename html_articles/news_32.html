At the scene of a school roof collapse in Pakistan

A school roof has collapsed in the north-western Pakistani city of Peshawar.

At least 33 pupils and a teacher were killed according to hospital officials, and more than a dozen were injured.

Announcing the arrest of the school's owner, police chief Mian Saeed promised a thorough inquiry into "whether there were any violations of building codes".

The BBC's Carrie Davis reports from the scene.
