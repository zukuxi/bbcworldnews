What you need to know if you're travelling to France or Spain

Hundreds of thousands of people have been evacuated across France and Spain as the countries have been affected by large wildfires.

The BBC's cost of living correspondent Kevin Peachey explains what to do if you're planning a trip to an area affected by wildfires.

UK travellers are encouraged to monitor the latest travel advice on the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO) website.
