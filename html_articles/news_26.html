Watch: The ups and downs of SpaceX's 13 Starship test flights

SpaceX is planning to carry out its 14th Starship rocket test flight on Monday.

If successful, it will be the first Starship to go in orbit around the Earth and the furthest one has ever flown.

The mission is set to complete approximately six orbits around the planet over a 10-hour flight.

But as the BBC's Ricky Boleto reports, there have been many ups and downs along the way.
