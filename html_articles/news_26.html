Watch: Moment Duane 'Keffe D' Davis is found guilty of Tupac Shakur's murder

Duane "Keffe D" Davis, a 63-year-old former gang boss, has been found guilty of murdering rapper Tupac Shakur.

The famous rapper was shot multiple times on 7 September 1996 in a drive-by shooting in Las Vegas and died several days later. Davis was unexpectedly charged with murder in 2023 and has been standing trial in Las Vegas.

Davis was charged with murder with a deadly weapon. He faces a potential sentence of life in prison without parole.
