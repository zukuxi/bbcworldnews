Video shows damaged Amazon cargo plane after crash

原文链接：https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/c62k8dz8r4lo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss

Watch: Video shows damaged Amazon cargo plane after crash

A Boeing 767-300 cargo plane was landing in Miami after departing from Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport in San Juan, Puerto Rico on Sunday when it overran the runway.

Five people are confirmed to have died and five more were injured in the crash.

The Amazon Prime Air flight struck "multiple vehicles" on the road, the local fire rescue told the BBC's US news partner CBS.

The aircraft then caught on fire, as more than 60 members of the Miami Dade Fire Rescue responded to the scene.

The airport closed all runways and issued a full ground stop after the incident.
