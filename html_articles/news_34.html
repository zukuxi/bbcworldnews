Baby Shark boy set to make K-pop debut

At just seven years old, Park Geon-roung starred in the Baby Shark music video that went on to become the most-watched YouTube clip of all time, with 17 billion views - and counting. Now, Baby Shark Boy wants to be a K-pop star.

He's backed by Pinkfong, the South Korean kids entertainment company that made Baby Shark - but will they find success again? The BBC's Tessa Wong explains everything you need to know about his upcoming debut.
