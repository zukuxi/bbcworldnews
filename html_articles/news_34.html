Moment drone hits supermarket in Odesa

CCTV video verified by the BBC shows the moment of a drone strike on a supermarket in the town of Mayaky in Ukraine's Odesa region.

The drone can be seen flying into the roof of the Tavria supermarket and exploding on impact.

Three people were injured in the attack, according to President Volodymyr Zelensky, who blamed Russia for the attack.

Russia's war in Ukraine is in its fifth year, which has been raging since Russia launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022.
