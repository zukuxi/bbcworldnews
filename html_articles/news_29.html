What it was like inside court for Tupac Shakur’s murder trial verdict

After just two hours of deliberation, a jury found 63-year-old Duane "Keffe D" Davis guilty of murdering rapper Tupac Shakur in 1996.

BBC presenter and Fame Under Fire podcast host Anoushka Mutanda-Dougherty describes what it was like inside the courtroom when the verdict was read out.
