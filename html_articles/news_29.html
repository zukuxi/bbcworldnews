Blocked roads and devastation as BBC drives to site of Nepal floods

The BBC has seen widespread devastation on the way from Nepal's capital of Kathmandu to Trishuli, one of the areas worst-hit by flooding on the Tibetan border.

As well as ambulances, hearses were also seen along the route, where rescuers said entire villages had been washed away by floodwaters.

South Asia correspondent Azadeh Moshiri reports.
