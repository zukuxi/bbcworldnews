'She's back': Fans dance, sing and cry after Céline Dion's comeback concert in Paris

Céline Dion's first concert in years delighted fans, with the Canadian singer crying as she made an emotional comeback to the stage.

It was Dion's first full concert in six years after she was diagnosed with a rare neurological disorder called stiff person syndrome, which forced her to cancel tours.

Joyful fans spilled out of the Paris La Défense Arena on Saturday night singing and dancing to her classic hits, while some were still wiping away tears.

Paris correspondent Hugh Schofield went along and reflects on getting swept up in the Dion dalliance that French fans are calling "the biggest comeback since Charles de Gaulle".
