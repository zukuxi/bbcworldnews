Watch: At the scene of Berlin Pride attack as manhunt continues

A manhunt is underway in Berlin after a vehicle drove into a crowded pride event, killing one person and injuring 16.

Police have named the suspect as 21-year-old Abdul B and have released an image of him.

A vehicle suspected of being used in the attack has been located by police inside the city's Tiergarten park.
