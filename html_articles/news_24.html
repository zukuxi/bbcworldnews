Video shows scale of flash flood hitting Nepal-Tibet border

Video shows the moment a deadly massive flash flood and mudslide hit the Nepal-Tibet border.

At least 31 people have been killed, and hundreds of tourists are missing.

The first two video clips have been edited by the BBC with white flashes, due to the distressing content.

Other clips show bridges being torn apart by the floodwater as people run to find cover.
