How the trial of two murdered Australian surfers ended in a guilty verdict

The mother of two murdered Australian surfers, who were killed in Mexico two years ago alongside their American friend, told a court "accountability matters".

Three men have been found guilty of murdering brothers Jake and Callum Robinson, and their US friend Carter Rhoad during a surfing trip in Mexico in 2024.

BBC's Will Grant takes a look at the case.
