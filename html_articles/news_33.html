USS Lincoln finally docks in San Diego after 10-month mission

Families have welcomed home the thousands of crew members on board the USS Abraham Lincoln after the warship spent more than 300 days at sea.

The aircraft carrier docked in San Diego after one of the longest deployments ever recorded, where it had been supporting the US-Israel war with Iran.

BBC Verify has been tracking its time at sea - as Jake Horton reports.
