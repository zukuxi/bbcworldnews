Watch: Nasa astronauts conduct 6.5-hour spacewalk outside ISS

Nasa astronauts Anil Menon and Jessica Meir ventured outside the International Space Station for a spacewalk on Thursday to install mounting hardware. The hardware will prepare the station’s 3B power channel for the future installation of new roll-out solar arrays.

The spacewalk lasted 6.5 hours.
