Watch: Jury still unable to reach unanimous decision in Lindsay Clancy case

For a second time in two days, jurors in the trial of Lindsay Clancy told the judge they could not agree on a verdict.

Clancy is a 36-year-old Massachusetts mother and former labour and delivery nurse who is on trial for the murder of her three young children in 2023. She is accused of strangling her kids - Cora, five, Dawson, three, and Callan, who was eight months - in their Massachusetts home.
